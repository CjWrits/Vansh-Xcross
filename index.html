Engineering precision that activates exclusively on open flame contact without false triggers from heat, impact, or ambient temperature.

01

When open flames touch the ball's outer casing, high-speed non-toxic micro-fuses ignite the internal core within 2 to 3 seconds.

Trigger: > 280°C Direct Flame
02

The lightweight casing safely ruptures outward with low kinetic force, creating a dense, spherical cloud of active extinguishing agent.

Dispersion: 360° Radial Sphere
03

Oxygen Depletion & Smothering

Frequently Answered Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Got questions about fire extinguishing balls? Here are straight, transparent answers.

Can the ball accidentally explode if dropped on the floor or bumped?

No, absolutely not. Vansh Fire Xcross utilizes specialized pyrotechnic micro-fuses that ignite only upon direct contact with naked flames or intense direct thermal fire (>280°C). It is completely inert and immune to accidental drops, heavy vibrations, mechanical shocks, and ambient room heat.

Is the chemical powder harmful or toxic to children, pets, or electronics?

No. The core extinguishing agent is ultra-pure Monoammonium Phosphate (MAP 90%), which is non-toxic, eco-friendly, non-hazardous to humans and household pets, and biodegradable. After discharge, the dry powder residue can simply be vacuumed or swept away with a damp cloth.

Does it need batteries, electrical wiring, or periodic gas refilling?

Zero power and zero maintenance. Vansh Fire Xcross is 100% autonomous. It has no pressure gauges to leak, no mechanical valves to jam, and requires no batteries or wiring. It remains fully armed 24/7/365 for its full 5-year guaranteed shelf life.

Which classes of fire is Vansh Fire Xcross certified to extinguish?

Vansh Fire Xcross is effective on all major standard fire classes:
• Class A: Solid combustibles (wood, paper, textiles, plastic)
• Class B: Flammable liquids (petrol, diesel, oils, solvents, paint)
• Class C: Flammable gases (LPG, CNG, propane, acetylene)
• Class E / Electrical: Energized electrical distribution boards, transformers, and inverters up to 5000V.

How high above the hazard should the mounting bracket be installed?

For optimum passive 24/7 protection, install the provided mounting bracket approximately 15 to 30 cm directly above the target hazard (such as an electrical panel breaker, gas cylinder manifold, or stove hood). Rising flames will immediately make contact and trigger 360° suppression.
