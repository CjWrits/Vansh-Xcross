STAGE 1: INITIATION

Pyrotechnic Micro-Fuse Ignition

Unlike thermal glass bulb sprinklers that rely solely on ambient room heat buildup, Vansh Fire Xcross integrates high-speed pyrotechnic micro-fuses distributed across the circumference of the sphere.

  • Trigger Mechanism: Direct contact with open flame (>280°C).
  • Reaction Latency: 2.5 to 3.8 seconds from flame touch to burst.
  • Immunity to False Triggers: Safe against ambient summer heat (up to 85°C), sunlight, humidity, and mechanical drops.

STAGE 2: CHEMICAL CHAIN BREAK

How MAP 90% Smothers the Fire

When the core detonates, the compressed ultra-fine ABC dry powder aerosolizes into a 360° spherical barrier:

1. Free Radical Neutralization: The phosphate radicals capture free hydrogen (H+) and hydroxyl (OH-) radicals in the flame, breaking the combustion chain instantly.
2. Oxygen Starvation Blanket: The powder creates a dense physical barrier between ambient oxygen (O₂) and the combustible fuel.
3. Endothermic Cooling Effect: Absorbs massive amounts of heat as it decomposes, cooling the burning base below its re-ignition flashpoint.
STAGE 3: OCCUPANT EVACUATION

The 120+ dB Acoustic Alarm

Most fatal residential fire casualties occur at night due to silent smoke inhalation while victims sleep. When Vansh Fire Xcross bursts, the controlled expansion creates a loud, resonant "pop" measured at 120 dB to 138 dB.

This sound wave serves as a life-saving warning siren, penetrating through closed doors to immediately awaken sleeping occupants and alert building security.
