Our Identity & Mission

Reimagining Fire Protection

Vansh Industries (Fire Xcross™) was founded with a singular conviction: No human life or vital business asset should ever be lost to a fire due to panic, lack of specialized training, or unmaintained equipment.

THE REAL-WORLD PROBLEM

Why Traditional Fire Cylinders Fail When Needed Most

In 92% of domestic and commercial fire incidents, victims experience instant panic. Traditional metal fire extinguishers require reading complex instruction labels, unlocking safety pins, aiming high-pressure nozzles, and walking directly toward scorching radiant heat.

Furthermore, over 65% of installed fire cylinders fail during real emergencies due to lost gas pressure, expired powder cakes, or skipped annual maintenance routines.

The Fire Xcross Solution: Simply toss the ball from 5 meters away or mount it on the included bracket for 24/7 autonomous protection. It activates on its own within 3 to 5 seconds upon flame contact.
