THE CORE PROBLEM

Why Traditional Fire Safety Fails When Needed Most

In 92% of domestic and commercial fire incidents, victims panic. Traditional metal fire extinguishers require reading complex instruction labels, unlocking safety pins, aiming high-pressure nozzles, and approaching scorching radiant heat.

Furthermore, over 65% of installed fire cylinders fail during real emergencies due to lost gas pressure, expired powder cakes, or skipped annual maintenance routines.
